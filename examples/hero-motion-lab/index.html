[ THE METHOD ] Motion notes

Less movement.
More meaning.

Motion should sharpen the idea, not ask for attention on its own. A few practical rules keep the first frame working for everyone.

01

Static first.

Every headline, action, and visual is present in the document. Animation adds timing; it never carries essential content.

02

One clear beat.

Copy, CTA, and art move as a short sequence. One purposeful accent connects motion to the visual concept.

03

Choice, always.

Keyboard focus stays visible. Reduced-motion preferences skip the entrance entirely. If GSAP is missing, the complete static design remains.
